## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that you have a **Quality Management System** that drives continuous improvement on engagements, not just a policy that sits on a shelf.

**Typical questions:**

- Where is your QMS policy?
- How do you collect and review customer satisfaction data?
- What is the escalation path when an engagement goes off-track?
- Who reviews quality data and when?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

- [ ] **Quality Management System policy** — PDF / Word
- [ ] **CSAT / NPS survey process** documented and a most recent run attached — PDF / Word / Excel
- [ ] **Escalation procedure** — PDF / Word
- [ ] **Evidence of management review** of quality data (minutes / decisions) — PDF / Word
- [ ] **ISO 9001 certificate** (optional, strong but not required) — PDF

{{% alert type="tip" %}}
Hypercare exit reviews are a great source of evidence — they combine CSAT, defect summary, and a
signed sign-off in a single document. Attach the last 2–3 anonymised hypercare exit memos.
{{% /alert %}}

---

## Evidence Guidance

### QMS Policy

Cover scope, objectives, ownership, review cadence, links to CSAT and escalation procedures, and the continuous-improvement loop. Sign and date the document at the top.

### CSAT / NPS

Provide the documented process (how often, what tool, who reviews) plus the most recent run's results across at least one engagement. Score is less important than evidence of the loop.

### Escalation Procedure

Document the levels (project → programme → executive), the trigger criteria, the timing SLAs, and the named roles. For SAP-on-Azure, also document the joint escalation path with Microsoft (PDM, FastTrack) and with SAP (CSM, MaxAttention) when applicable.

## Common Gaps

- **CSAT data is ad-hoc:** add a documented cadence and a most recent run.
- **Escalation procedure has no SLAs:** add response and resolution times per level.
- **No management review evidence:** include minutes / decisions from a quarterly quality review.
