Module A covers the general organisational controls that every Microsoft Advanced Specialization
audit assesses — legal identity, financial standing, delivery methodology, quality management,
customer satisfaction, complaint handling, and security and privacy posture.

Evidence collected here is reusable across every specialization your organisation pursues, so keep
it in a shared, dated evidence library rather than rebuilding it each cycle.
