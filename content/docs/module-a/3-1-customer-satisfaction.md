## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that you have **measurable, positive customer outcomes** — not just a happy reference. Trends matter.

**Typical questions:**

- Do you collect CSAT / NPS systematically?
- Can you show results across multiple customers?
- Do you have at least two reference customers willing to attest publicly or privately?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

- [ ] **CSAT / NPS results** across at least 3 customers — PDF / Excel
- [ ] **At least 2 reference customer testimonials** or signed letters — PDF
- [ ] **Methodology** for collecting and reviewing CSAT — PDF / Word
- [ ] **Trend over at least 12 months** — PDF / Excel chart

{{% alert type="tip" %}}
SAP customers often won't agree to public references. Anonymised testimonial letters on customer
letterhead (signed) are accepted by most auditors.
{{% /alert %}}

---

## Evidence Guidance

### CSAT / NPS Results

Provide the raw export plus a one-page summary chart. Trend matters — auditors prefer 12 months of data even at low volume over a single high score.

### Testimonials

For each testimonial, capture: customer (or "Customer A"), engagement scope, business outcome, signed by (role + date).

### Methodology

Describe survey timing (end of phase, end of engagement, post-hypercare), tool used, who reviews, and how findings feed back into the QMS.

## Common Gaps

- **Only one customer's data:** survey at least three engagements before requesting the audit.
- **No public references:** anonymised, signed letters are accepted — get them on customer letterhead.
- **No trend:** even sparse monthly data points are better than a single snapshot.
