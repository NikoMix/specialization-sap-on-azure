The guide is organised in the order you will work through it. **Overview**, **Pre-Qualification
Requirements** and **Audit Process** set the context. **Module A** and **Module B** carry the
control-by-control evidence guidance. The **Engagement Playbook** is the delivery motion that
produces that evidence, and **Innersource** explains how to contribute improvements back.
