## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that **at least one individual** in your organisation has the documented SAP-on-Azure skilling, satisfied by **either** route:

1. Holds the **Microsoft Certified: Azure for SAP Workloads Specialty** (**AZ-120**) certification, **or**
2. Has completed the **Run SAP on the Microsoft Cloud** learning path on Microsoft Learn.

**Typical questions:**

- Show me the Microsoft Learn transcript.
- Has the skilling been validated in Partner Center?
- Is the holder still employed and available?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

- [ ] **AZ-120 holder Microsoft Learn transcript** (PDF export from learn.microsoft.com profile) — PDF, **OR**
- [ ] **Run-SAP-on-Microsoft-Cloud learning-path completion certificate** — PDF
- [ ] **Skilling mapping table** (Name → AZ-120 or learning path → completion date → expiry) — Excel / PDF
- [ ] **Partner Center validation screenshot** (where the skilling shows in your Partner Center profile) — PDF

{{% alert type="tip" %}}
AZ-120 carries the most weight with customers. If you're using the learning-path route to pass
this cycle, book AZ-120 for the holder for the next cycle — it's a small ask and significantly
strengthens future sales conversations.
{{% /alert %}}

## Evidence Guidance

### Pulling the Transcript

The holder signs in to **learn.microsoft.com → Profile → Certifications / Achievements** and exports as PDF. Include the date in the filename: `B3.1_AZ120_Transcript_NameSurname_2026-03-01.pdf`.

### Validating in Partner Center

Microsoft now validates AZ-120 and the learning-path completion directly in Partner Center — capture a screenshot of the skilling card on your Partner Center membership page.

## Common Gaps

- **Holder has left the company:** book another team member onto AZ-120 immediately or have them complete the learning path.
- **Transcript not validated in Partner Center:** ensure the holder's Microsoft Learn profile is linked to their Partner Center identity.
- **No mapping table:** add a short Excel — auditors expect to see the link between people and evidence.
