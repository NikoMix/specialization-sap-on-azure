## When to use this

First meeting with a prospect, or anywhere you need to leave a single artefact that explains *what we do and why on Azure*. Tailor the variant to the conversation.

## Variants

Three flavours of the same offering — pick one (or combine):

| Variant | Customer signal | Headline outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Greenfield S/4HANA on Azure** | New SAP investment, no existing ERP debt | Validated S/4HANA architecture on Azure, signed cutover and DR runbook, hypercare exit |
| **Brownfield migration (ECC → Azure, ECC → S/4HANA on Azure)** | Existing ECC / R/3, end of HW lease, S/4HANA conversion forced | Like-for-like migration with optional database conversion, signed cutover and rollback plan |
| **RISE with SAP — Azure landing zone** | Customer chose RISE; partner owns Azure-side | Production-ready Azure landing zone, peering, identity integration, monitoring hand-off, RACI with SAP ECS |

## Inputs you need from the customer

- Current SAP estate (products, versions, DB, sizing)
- Target architecture preference (greenfield S/4HANA, brownfield, RISE)
- RTO / RPO commitments per system
- Regulatory and data-residency constraints
- Go-live target date

## Step-by-step

<Steps>
1. Open the PPTX template and replace placeholder text for company name and customer.
2. Pick **one** variant section and remove the other two (or leave all three for an "options" pitch).
3. Drop in the customer's logo + your team's headshots.
4. Update the pricing model placeholder (T&amp;M / fixed-price per wave) with your commercial team.
5. Export to PDF for distribution.
</Steps>

## Output: customer-ready deliverable

A single 2-page PDF, your-branded, that the customer leaves the meeting with.
