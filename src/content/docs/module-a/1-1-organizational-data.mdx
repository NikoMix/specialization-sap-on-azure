## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation is a **legally registered entity** with a clear organisational structure and identifiable key personnel responsible for SAP-on-Azure service delivery.

**Typical questions:**

- Is the company legally registered in its operating jurisdiction?
- Is there a documented organisational structure showing reporting lines?
- Are key personnel (leadership, SAP practice lead, Azure infra lead, account managers) identifiable?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

- [ ] **Certificate of Incorporation** or equivalent legal registration document — PDF
- [ ] **Company registration number** visible in a document — PDF / screenshot
- [ ] **Organisational chart** showing reporting lines and key roles — PDF / PowerPoint / Visio
- [ ] **List of key personnel** with names, titles, and responsibilities — PDF / Word / Excel
- [ ] **Registered address** matching official company records — PDF / screenshot

<Aside type="tip">
  Export the Companies House (UK) / Chamber of Commerce / equivalent registry entry as PDF — this
  satisfies the registration number, registered address, and incorporation items in a single document.
</Aside>

## Evidence Guidance

### Certificate of Incorporation

Obtain from the national business registry in your operating jurisdiction:

- 🇬🇧 UK: [Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/companies-house)
- 🇩🇪 Germany: Handelsregister
- 🇳🇱 Netherlands: KvK (Kamer van Koophandel)
- 🇺🇸 USA: State Secretary of State filing
- Other: National business registry or notarised equivalent

### Organisational Chart

The chart should clearly show:

- Executive / leadership layer
- **SAP practice** (Basis, functional, ABAP development)
- **Azure infrastructure practice** (cloud architects, landing-zone team, networking)
- Delivery management / project management office
- Account management / customer success

**Recommended tools:** Microsoft Visio, PowerPoint SmartArt, or draw.io exported as PDF.

### Key Personnel List

Prepare a table with at minimum:

| Name | Title | Practice (SAP / Azure / Delivery) | Responsibility | Microsoft Certification(s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

## Common Gaps

- **Org chart doesn't show SAP / Azure split:** add a second tier showing the SAP and Azure practices distinctly.
- **Certificate is more than 3 years old:** request a current extract from the registry.
- **Key personnel list is informal (email thread):** formalise into a signed Word or PDF document.
