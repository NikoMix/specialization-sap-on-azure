## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that you have a **documented, actively maintained** information security and data protection posture. SAP customers are particularly sensitive to security — GxP, financial data, and PII all routinely live in SAP systems.

**Typical questions:**

- Is your InfoSec policy aligned to ISO 27001, SOC 2, or NIST?
- How do you handle GDPR / regional data protection?
- What's your breach response procedure?
- How is staff trained?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

- [ ] **Information Security policy** (ISO 27001 / SOC 2 / NIST mapped) — PDF / Word
- [ ] **Data protection / GDPR statement** — PDF / Word
- [ ] **Breach response procedure** — PDF / Word
- [ ] **Staff security training records** (most recent 12 months) — PDF / Excel
- [ ] **ISO 27001 certificate / SOC 2 report** (if held — strongest evidence) — PDF

<Aside type="tip">
  An active ISO 27001 certificate fully satisfies this control. Attach the certificate plus a
  one-page scope statement; the auditor will not require deeper evidence in most cases.
</Aside>

---

## Evidence Guidance

### InfoSec Policy

The policy must reference the underlying framework, define ownership, set review cadence, and be signed/dated by the policy owner.

### Data Protection

If you operate in the EU/EEA/UK, attach your GDPR statement, your DPA template, and the appointed DPO contact. For SAP customers specifically, document how you handle customer data during transports, refreshes, and copies for non-production environments.

### Breach Response

Cover detection, containment, customer notification SLAs, regulator notification SLAs (e.g. 72-hour GDPR), post-incident review.

### Staff Training

Provide training matrix per role + completion records for the last 12 months. Include SAP-specific items (Basis admins, ABAP developers handling personal data).

## Common Gaps

- **Policy isn't mapped to a framework:** add a one-page mapping table to ISO 27001 controls.
- **No staff training records:** attach the LMS export for the last 12 months.
- **GDPR statement missing for EU work:** attach the template and DPO contact.
