## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation is **financially stable** and **adequately insured** to deliver SAP-on-Azure engagements — where a single incident can carry meaningful customer impact.

**Typical questions:**

- Are your financial statements available and current?
- Is professional indemnity insurance active with sufficient cover?
- Are you financially healthy enough to support a multi-month SAP engagement?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

- [ ] **Most recent set of financial statements** (audited where available) — PDF
- [ ] **Professional indemnity insurance certificate** (active, with cover amount visible) — PDF
- [ ] **Public liability insurance certificate** (if applicable) — PDF
- [ ] **Statement of financial health** signed by CFO or equivalent — PDF / Word

<Aside type="caution">
  Professional indemnity insurance is a **hard gate** — gaps here will block the audit. Engage your
  broker weeks in advance if cover needs to be put in place or extended.
</Aside>

---

## Evidence Guidance

### Financial Statements

Provide the most recent year-end statements. If you operate under audit-exempt thresholds, a directors' report plus management accounts is usually acceptable — confirm with the audit firm at kickoff.

### Professional Indemnity Insurance

The certificate must show:

- Insured party (must match your registered legal entity)
- Policy number
- Cover amount (sufficient for the scale of your SAP engagements — typically &ge; USD 1M)
- Policy period (active across the entire audit window)

### CFO Statement (optional but recommended)

A short signed letter from the CFO confirming financial standing speeds up auditor review where statements are abbreviated.

## Common Gaps

- **PI cover expires inside the audit window:** renew before requesting the audit; many policies renew with overlap.
- **Cover amount below the auditor's expectation:** increase cover or provide a sign-off from the customer that lower cover is accepted.
- **Statements are too old:** provide year-end + management accounts to bridge the gap.
