## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies you have a **repeatable customer onboarding pack** — not bespoke per engagement, but a productised set of templates and a migration playbook covering the full lifecycle from SOW to hypercare exit.

**Typical questions:**

- Where is your SAP-on-Azure migration playbook?
- Show me your High-Level Design / Low-Level Design / runbook / Knowledge Transfer / hypercare templates.
- Has this pack been applied to at least one real customer?

</Aside>

## Evidence Guidance

### Migration / Build Playbook

The playbook should cover SAP Activate phases mapped to the Azure workstream, with the deliverable templates referenced at the right phase. Reference [Engagement Playbook → Discovery Workshop](../../engagement/discovery-workshop/) and [Reference Architectures](../../engagement/reference-architectures/) as the inputs.

### High-Level Design and Low-Level Design

Use the downloadable [High-Level Design (HLD) template](../../engagement/deliverables/hld-template/) and [Low-Level Design (LLD) template](../../engagement/deliverables/lld-template/) as the baseline. The auditor wants to see:

- Landing zone topology
- SAP application landscape
- HANA HA topology and DR
- Network + identity design
- VM SKUs per tier, ANF volumes, disk layout
- HSR config, Pacemaker config, ASCS/ERS cluster, backup policies

### Runbook, Knowledge Transfer, and Hypercare

Templates available at:

- [Runbook template](../../engagement/deliverables/runbook-template/)
- [Knowledge Transfer plan template](../../engagement/deliverables/kt-plan-template/)
- [Hypercare plan template](../../engagement/deliverables/hypercare-plan-template/)

### Evidence of Application

A single anonymised cover slide from a filled artefact (e.g. "Customer A S/4HANA on Azure — HLD v1.2") is enough.

## Common Gaps

- **Templates are old or inconsistent:** swap to the latest downloadable versions in `public/templates/deliverables/`.
- **No evidence of application:** attach an anonymised cover slide or signed sign-off.
- **No SAP Activate mapping:** add a one-page mapping in the playbook intro.
