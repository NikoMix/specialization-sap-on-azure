## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that **at least three unique customers** are contributing to Azure Consumed Revenue across SAP-on-Azure-eligible services in the trailing 3 months, via **DPOR, PAL, or CSP** attribution.

**Typical questions:**

- Show me a list of the customers attributed to your ACR.
- For each, what's the attribution type (DPOR / PAL / CSP)?
- Are these the same customers as your case studies in B.1.1?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

- [ ] **Unique customer list** (≥ 3) with anonymised customer ID and country — PDF / Excel
- [ ] **PAL / DPOR / CSP attribution evidence** per customer — PDF / screenshot
- [ ] **Cross-reference table** linking each customer to a B.1.1 case study (where applicable) — PDF / Excel

<Aside type="tip">
  Government subscriptions are **not** eligible for ACR via PAL. If a customer is sovereign, confirm
  attribution path with your PDM before counting them toward the 3.
</Aside>

---

## Evidence Guidance

### Customer List

Use the Partner Center **By Customer** view of the ACR export. Replace customer names with "Customer A / B / C…" if confidentiality requires; keep the customer tenant ID for the auditor's verification.

### PAL Setup (fast fix for under-attribution)

PAL can be configured same-day per customer subscription — have the customer admin add your team's UPN under **Subscriptions → Access control (IAM)**. Document each PAL with a screenshot.

### Cross-Reference

Map each of the 3 customers to:

- The case study in B.1.1 (if delivered)
- The skilling holder (B.3.1) who led the engagement
- The audit-readiness evidence (B.4.1) folder

## Common Gaps

- **Only 2 unique customers:** establish PAL on one more customer subscription — same-day possible.
- **Customers attributed to non-SAP services:** confirm spend is on SAP-eligible services per [B.2.1](../2-1-acr-performance/).
- **Government subscription counted toward 3:** swap for a commercial-tenant customer.
