## Lifecycle

Every piece of content (page, template, reference architecture) moves through four states:

| State | Meaning | Visible? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Draft** | New or substantially changed; under review | Yes, with a "Draft" callout at the top |
| **Reviewed** | Practice-lead reviewed; merged | Yes |
| **Endorsed** | Applied successfully on at least one customer engagement; recommended | Yes |
| **Deprecated** | Superseded by a newer version; kept for back-reference | Yes, with a "Deprecated" callout linking to the replacement |

## Who endorses

| Content area | Endorser |
| :--- | :--- |
| Audit controls (Module A / B) | Audit compliance lead |
| Engagement playbook | SAP-on-Azure practice lead |
| Reference architectures | SAP reference architects |
| Deliverable templates | SAP delivery leads |
| Innersource governance | Innersource stewards |

See `CODEOWNERS` at the repo root for current owners.

## Review cadence

- **Quarterly** — practice leads review their content area for drift (new Azure services, new SAP releases, new audit cycle requirements).
- **Per audit cycle** — full audit-content sweep before the annual issue refresh.
- **Per engagement** — consultants flag any content that didn't hold up in the field via the `lesson-learned` issue template.

## How to propose a change to Endorsed content

1. Open a PR with the change.
2. Reference the engagement / customer (anonymised) where the change was validated.
3. The endorser reviews. If accepted, the change ships and content re-enters the Endorsed state.

## Deprecation

When superseding content:

<Aside type="caution">
  Add a deprecation callout linking to the replacement at the top of the deprecated page. Do not
  delete deprecated pages for at least one full audit cycle — partners may have linked to them
  from old evidence packs.
</Aside>
