## Lessons-learned backlog

Open `lesson-learned` issues are surfaced here at quarterly governance reviews — top votes get promoted into the active themes above.

## How to propose a new theme

Open a `template-improvement` or `lesson-learned` issue describing the gap. If it gains support in the next quarterly governance review, it moves into Active themes with an assigned owner.

<Aside type="tip">
  The roadmap is intentionally short — too many themes dilutes focus. If your idea isn't on it
  today, open a lesson-learned issue so it's captured for the next governance review.
</Aside>
